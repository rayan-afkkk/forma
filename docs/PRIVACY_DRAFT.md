## Summary

Forma works without an account. Your workouts, preferences and equipment are stored **only on your
device**. We do not run servers that receive this information. The app contains no advertising,
analytics or tracking SDKs.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Profile:**
  - the name you choose (optional);
  - goal, experience level and usual session length;
  - preferences such as quiet mode and limited space.
- **Equipment profiles, excluded exercises, favourites and schedule.**
- **Workout history:** exercises performed, sets, reps, times, weights, how sessions felt, and
  optional notes.
- **App settings:** theme, units, sound, haptics, keep screen on, reminders.
- **Subscription status:** the last confirmed status from Google Play, cached so the app works
  offline.

Some of this information, such as how a workout felt, may be considered health-related in some
places. It stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

## What leaves your device

- **Google Play Billing.** If you subscribe, Google processes the payment under Google's privacy
  policy. The app asks Google Play whether a subscription is active. We receive no payment card
  details.
- **Android backup (your choice).** If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may
  include the app's data in your Google account backup and in device-to-device transfer. The
  cached subscription status is excluded. You control this in system settings.
- **Files you export.** Exported backup (JSON) and CSV files go wherever you choose to save them.
- **Email you send to us**, if you contact support.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Product events

The app defines a small set of product events:

- onboarding completed;
- first workout started;
- first workout completed;
- exercise replaced;
- exclusion added;
- session resumed;
- paywall viewed;
- purchase completed.

These events carry no workout content, names or identifiers. In this version **they are not sent
anywhere**: development builds write them to the device log, and release builds discard them. If
aggregate analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before release, and the change
will be opt-in where required.

## Notifications

Workout reminders are off by default. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for notification
permission only when you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on the device.

## Your choices

- **Export:** You › Backup and export.
- **Delete:** You › Delete local data removes your profile, preferences, equipment and workout
  history from the device. The cached subscription status is kept, because it reflects your Google
  Play purchase. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
- **Subscriptions** are managed in Google Play.
